## 1. Two mathematical defects, corrected

### 1.1 `rig_covariance` returned a conditional covariance

The joint covariance is `C = H^{-1}` over rig + board poses. The required
uncertainty of the rig parameters is the **marginal** block `C_rr`; the published
code returned `C_rr − C_rv C_vv^{-1} C_vr`, which conditions on the poses and is
far too small. On a 10-view synthetic problem:

| quantity | fx sigma |
|---|---:|
| marginal `C_rr` | **0.584 px** |
| conditional (old) | 0.0537 px |
| ratio | **0.092** |

The marginal information is computed as the Schur complement of the weighted
Jacobian, `S = H_rr − H_rv H_vv^{+} H_vr`, with `H_vv^{+}` taken at the
singular-value scale of `J_v` (not the `H` eigenvalue scale). The regression
test verifies `S = inv(C_rr)·σ²` to 1e-6 relative for the plain estimator.

### 1.2 `rescaled` multiplied the whole covariance by `s²`

Only translations scale; intrinsics, distortion and rotations do not. The
correct transform is `C' = J C Jᵀ` with `J = diag(1,…,1,s,…,s,1,…)`, plus the
anchor terms. The test asserts intrinsic/rotation sigmas unchanged, translation
and baseline sigmas doubled at `s=2`, and the anchor sigma propagated.

### 1.3 Robust sandwich meat

The Huber sandwich is `A^{-1} B A^{-1}`, `A=JᵀWJ`,
`B=Jᵀ diag((w·r)²) J`. The published code used `w·r²` (one `w` too few), which
inflated the covariance for outliers. After the correction, synthetic NEES/n is
**0.91 / 1.01 / 0.92 / 1.02** at 5/8/12/20 views with `free_kc=False` (3
trials), i.e. calibrated. With `free_kc=True` the 60-trial audit gives
**1.17–1.32** and 68 % coverage 0.64–0.68: mildly optimistic.

## 2. Stopping is now conservative and explainable

`calib.quality.stopping_report` requires **all** of:

* ≥10 views, ≥3 distance bins, both tilt signs and ≥10° range each axis;
* ≥75 % image-quadrant coverage and ≥300 observations per camera;
* full Jacobian rank and condition ≤1e7;
* marginal sigma thresholds for fx, baseline, IR rotation, RGB translation;
* an **independent held-out** reprojection ≤ the target;
* parameter stability: max |Δ|/σ < 4 when dropping the newest view.

Covariance thresholds alone can never accept. Presets: `fast` (1.25 px / 0.20 mm
/ 0.8 px held-out), `standard` (0.75 / 0.12 / 0.5), `high` (0.35 / 0.06 / 0.35).
Every check reports value, limit, pass and `reasons_failed`.

On the 25-view vendor corpus: **standard passes** (σfx 0.52 px, baseline σ
0.05 mm, held-out 0.455 px, stability 1.13); **high fails** (σfx 0.52 > 0.35,
held-out 0.455 > 0.35). On real EXStar-prefix subsets, marginal fx σ is
**3.8–4.1 px at k=8**, 1.17–1.31 at 12, 0.74–0.89 at 20, 0.48–0.52 at 25.

## 2.1 Uncertainty audit: what the covariance cannot see

Corrected-core uncertainty results (`analysis/calibration/audit/uncertainty_report.md`):

* NEES/n is 0.91–1.02 with `free_kc=False` (3 trials). The final 60-trial
  audit with `free_kc=True` gives **1.17–1.32** and 68 % coverage 0.64–0.68:
  mildly optimistic; the plain `gn_mad` estimator is closer (1.03–1.08).
* **View composition** is a significant *pixel* uncertainty: the audit's
  resampling changes fx by 1.26–1.60 px while the reported sigma is 0.48–0.52
  px (ratio 0.32–0.44); a 6-fit drop-4 vendor bootstrap gives 0.647 vs 0.504 px
  (ratio 0.78). A 6-fit `bootstrap_sigma()` is available (`--bootstrap`);
  single-fit covariance alone is not always conservative enough for production.
* **Plate-pitch error is the dominant overconfidence:** +1 % true pitch with
  nominal pitch assumed gives a 1.81 mm baseline error against a 0.046 mm
  reported sigma (median |z| 39.3); +0.1 % is already ~4σ. Pitch is a scale
  gauge, not pixel noise. Anchor it with a measured length or pass a pitch
  sigma; the pixel covariance cannot detect it.
* **1 mm low-order board bow** gives fx |z| 6.6, cx 10.9, rotation 7.1; a
  10 % biased fixed distortion gives fx |z| 3.8–5.3, cx 11.9. Do not fix `kc`
  unless the factory values are verified to a few percent.
* **A camera with no observations** has zero variance and arbitrary error; the
  rank and per-camera coverage checks are mandatory. Partial visibility (60 %
  missing) is handled (z≈0.7); a 0.25 px global marker shift moves fx by ~1.3 px
  (z≈1.8) but leaves the baseline nearly unchanged.
* The quality report therefore adds an explicit `physical_scale` field: an
  unresolved pitch marks the absolute scale as **not physically validated**,
  even when the pixel-level stopping passes. Since 2026-11-10 the pitch is
  recovered from the vendor binaries (`vendor_model`, 28.0 mm), which the field
  now reports as `vendor_geometry: true` (distinct from an artefact
  measurement).

## 3. Colour record: real 17.66 mm error, stale record

Frame-invariant `t_rel(L→Tex)`:

| source | rotation | `t_rel` mm | ‖t‖ | Δ vs fit |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| shipped CCF | 17.005° | (−122.986, **16.894**, 17.134) | 125.318 | **17.66 mm**, 0.12° |
| our joint fit | 16.885° | (−121.078, −0.069, 12.626) | 121.735 | — |
| factory 22-double | 16.998° | (−122.241, −0.108, 12.486) | 122.877 | 1.17 mm, 0.11° |

The IR control is 0.76 mm / 0.18°. The 17.66 mm is a near-pure y shift, causing
**46.6 px mean** projected plate error (33.6–77.5 over 25 views, max 85.6). The
2026 block was written atomically, but `hasTexture:false` and the pose anomaly
indicate a stale/mixed-generation Tex record, not a decode/frame error. The
engine now warns via `calib.ccf.consistency` and never fits blindly to it; repair
needs a colour-calibration run on the unit.

Writer convention: E10 calls `Sn3D_Quar_CCF`/`CalibrationCCF::QuarCCF`
(0x1802e5e70), camera 1's first valid view is the shared world frame, cameras
2/3 are relative poses. `TribleCCF` (0x1802e6a20) has no importer in the dumped
modules.

## 4. Plate: 8×5 / 41 markers resolved; pitch resolved to 28.0 mm

* Device corpus (25 IR images) and video: **8×5**, 40 lattice nodes + 1
  off-lattice = **41 holes = 37 small + 4 large**. Three large on-lattice at
  (2,1),(4,1),(5,1); off-lattice at (3.5,3).
* DLL board drawing and manual UI: **8×7**, 56 lattice + 1 = **57 holes = 53
  small + 4 large**; large at (2,2),(4,2),(5,2),(3.5,4). Same design with rows
  0/6 added; `PlateSpec` supports both.
* **Resolved 2026-11-10 from the vendor binaries**: the calibration lattice is
  17 × 14 at **14.00 mm** (`iX/iY/iSpace` from `initComputeAlg`; generator
  `CalibrateBoard3DAlg::generate 0x18004BA90`) and markers occupy every second
  node ⇒ **28.0 mm marker pitch**. The per-board `AA`/`BB` distance constraints
  (150.804/√116 = 14.002, 125.071/√80 = 13.983) confirm the lattice; the plate
  outline is 8 × 6 marker pitches = 224 × 168 mm. `200x150` is a profile name,
  not a plate dimension, so the 25.0 mm hypothesis is retracted. The vendor CCF
  written on this corpus corresponds to a 27.72 mm effective scale (1.0 % below
  the nominal model) — a solver difference. See
  `docs/calibration/plate_spec_recovered.md`.
* No separate board-geometry blob is needed: the vendor generates the array
  from `iX/iY/iSpace` and pins its scale with the PLE `AA`/`BB` distances.

## 5. Detector and solver

* **Detector** (52 synthetic stress cases): Canny+sub-pixel edge and conic
  back-projection give 0.018–0.029 px synthetic RMS; centroid is 0.055 px under
  severe tilt and **0.544 px** with neighbouring holes. On the real vendor
  corpus the residual floor is model-limited, so centroid remains best
  (0.367 px). Recommendation: centroid seed → edge/conic refinement after the
  first homography; retire the threshold ellipse.
* **Solver** (vendor25, 189 params, `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`): SciPy dense
  9.44 s; SciPy+analytic 1.21 s (7.8×); native C++ LM **0.055 s (172×)**;
  OpenCV 0.137 s but a weaker rig-imposed problem (RMS 0.761 px). Ceres 2.2.0
  cannot compile (Eigen headers missing). Pin BLAS to one thread (3.4× free)
  and add the analytic Jacobian/covariance before considering the native path.

## 6. Capture-count conclusion (held-out view-count audit)

Design: 10 synthetic scenes × 5 random seeds × k∈{8,10,12,15,20,25}, 10
independent validation views never used for selection/training; real corpus
5-fold (5 held-out views, 20 training views); fixed/uniform/random/adaptive/
ground-truth-Fisher/oracle; **UCB95** not means. Engine hash f91efb1e
(post-freeze float32 init; free_kc=False unchanged). Full tables:
`analysis/calibration/audit/viewcount_results.json`.

### Synthetic ground truth — fx error, mean / UCB95 (px)

| selection | k=8 | k=12 | k=20 | k=25 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| fixed EXStar | 1.44 / 1.83 | 0.92 / 1.12 | 0.69 / 0.83 | 0.49 / 0.65 |
| uniform | **0.78 / 0.99** | 0.61 / 0.75 | 0.45 / 0.61 | 0.49 / 0.65 |
| random | 1.36 / 1.64 | 0.85 / 0.97 | 0.58 / 0.73 | 0.49 / 0.65 |
| adaptive | 0.89 / 1.14 | **0.72 / 0.85** | 0.47 / 0.61 | 0.49 / 0.65 |
| oracle (diagnostic) | 0.35 / 0.45 | 0.28 / 0.35 | 0.24 / 0.36 | 0.49 / 0.65 |

Minimum k whose **UCB95** meets the target:

| target | fixed | uniform | random | adaptive | oracle |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| fx ≤ 1.0 px | 15 | 8 | 12 | 12 | 8 |
| fx ≤ 0.5 px | not at 25 | not at 25 | not at 25 | not at 25 | 8 |
| baseline ≤ 0.05 mm | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 |
| held-out ≤ 0.5 px | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 |

Baseline and held-out reprojection saturate early; **intrinsics dominate the
capture count**. The oracle (selection with ground truth) shows that *which*
views are chosen matters more than the raw count: 8 oracle views beat 25 fixed
views for fx. Adaptive is consistently better than fixed at the same k
(8: 0.89 vs 1.44; 12: 0.72 vs 0.92) but does not reach the oracle.

### Real corpus — CCF-relative, held-out

| selection | k | mean max|Δfx| px | σfx px | held-out px | baseline Δ mm |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| fixed | 8 | 17.98* | 3.04 | 0.605 | 0.108 |
| fixed | 12 | 3.23 | 1.29 | 0.466 | 0.035 |
| fixed | 15 | 4.21 | 1.01 | 0.460 | 0.030 |
| uniform | 8 | 5.92 | 0.76 | 0.444 | 0.109 |
| uniform | 12 | 4.32 | 0.72 | 0.440 | 0.062 |
| random | 8 | 4.41 | 0.85 | 0.461 | 0.096 |
| random | 12 | 4.75 | 0.73 | 0.446 | 0.041 |
| adaptive | 8 | 5.06 | 0.89 | 0.486 | 0.050 |
| adaptive | 12 | 4.57 | 0.72 | 0.448 | 0.033 |
| full | 25 | 5.12 | 0.52 | 0.456 | 0.014 |

*fixed k=8 has one fold with a gross failure; the mean is not representative of
a good capture set. Δfx vs CCF is 4–6 px for every non-fixed method and does
**not** converge to zero as k grows: the CCF is a reference, not ground truth.

### Answer

**8–12 captures are not defensible as a universal replacement for 25.**
They can meet a **1 px fx** target with good selection (uniform 8, adaptive 12)
and they comfortably meet baseline/held-out targets, but they do **not** meet a
0.5 px intrinsic target (no method reaches the UCB95 target even at 25; only
oracle selection at 8 does). For the standard 0.75 px target, 12–20 views are
needed depending on selection; for a high 0.35 px target, the current fixed
design is insufficient at 25.

The defensible statement is: **the count is target- and selection-dependent.**
A coverage-constrained adaptive plan can approach the 1 px target with ~12
views; 20–25 are needed for 0.5 px mean accuracy; 8 only works for baseline/
held-out with generous intrinsics or an oracle-like selection. The earlier
"8–12" headline is withdrawn.

## 7. Remaining physical validation

1. Count the holes and caliper the pitch/outline; choose 8×5 or 8×7 and the
   pitch source.
2. Calibrate the user's unit; compare with the factory CCF; verify held-out and
   stability on real captures.
3. Re-run colour calibration with texture enabled; validate RGB registration on
   a textured object.
4. Measure a known artefact (plane/sphere/step gauge) at 300/400/500 mm; report
   physical depth error, not just reprojection.
5. Only then consider a device write; keep the factory snapshot and rollback.
