# Capture-strategy evaluation — Monte Carlo comparison

Status: 2026-11-10. Raw data: `analysis/experiments/strategy_bench.py` output
(`strategy_mc10.json`, 100 runs) and `session_modes.py` output
(`session_modes_small.json`).  Tables regenerated by
`analysis/experiments/analyze_strategy.py`.

## 1. What was compared

Ten strategies, each selecting from the same 110-pose candidate pool
(distances 240–560 mm, tilts ±20° in x and y, rolls 0°/90°, lateral offsets),
on the same synthetic scene and noise realisation per trial (common random
numbers):

| strategy | type | rule |
|---|---|---|
| `fixed_vendor` | offline fixed | EXStar's 5 orientations (0, ±18° about x/y) × 5 distances (240…560) = 25 |
| `uniform` | offline fixed | 12 evenly spread poses (3 distances × 3 tilts + 3 rolled) |
| `random_diverse` | offline random | random pool poses with a diversity rejection, 25 |
| `aprilcal` | online | greedy expected reprojection error over 48 target points (the AprilCal criterion, adapted to the circle plate) |
| `kalibr_param` | online | greedy D-optimal on the marginal rig-parameter covariance |
| `fisher_param` | online | greedy A-optimal on the marginal rig-parameter covariance |
| `task_volume` | online | greedy A-optimal on the 3-D covariance over the 200–600 mm volume |
| `task_effort` | online | `task_volume` with the operator-effort penalty in the utility |
| `task_depth` | online | greedy minimisation of depth variance |
| `hybrid` | online | coverage-first bootstrap (4 diverse views), then `task_effort` |

One trial = one new synthetic scene (random true intrinsics/rig) with 0.2 px
Gaussian noise, 2 % outliers at 8 px and 5 % missing markers.  Each run is
evaluated at 8, 12, 16 and 25 accepted views by re-solving on that prefix.

**Metrics.**  *3-D recon* is the model-induced error: 36 points over the volume
are projected with the true rig, 0.2 px noise is added, and the same pixels are
triangulated with the estimated rig; the error is against the true-rig
reconstruction, so noise cancels and only calibration error remains.  *Held-out
3-D* reconstructs six unseen board poses.  *Baseline rel err* is the scale
error (true baseline known).  *Rotation err* is the relative-camera angle error.
*Task σ* is the predicted median 3-D sigma of the estimated model (a
self-consistency number, not truth).  *RMS* is the training residual, reported
to show that it does not rank strategies.

## 3. What the comparison establishes

1. **Informative selection beats the fixed plan at every budget.**  At 25 views
   the task-space planner is 0.193 mm vs 0.380 mm for the vendor plan, wins
   10/10 trials, and the paired CI excludes zero.  The same holds for the
   parameter-covariance planners (10/10 and 9/10), so the gain is not an
   artefact of one objective.
2. **More captures are not automatically better.**  The vendor plan is
   *worse* at 25 views (0.380 mm) than at 8 (0.314 mm).  In this stress
   configuration the 5-term distortion is freed at ≥10 views; on the vendor
   poses the extra coefficients absorb noise and the 3-D error grows.  This is
   the concrete form of the mission's warning: additional images must be
   informative *and* the model must stay identifiable.  `task_volume` improves
   monotonically (0.262 → 0.230 → 0.193) because every added view is chosen to
   reduce the 3-D volume uncertainty.
3. **RMS does not rank strategies.**  All strategies sit at 1.6 px training RMS
   (the injected outliers dominate it), while their 3-D error differs by 2×.
   This is the Rojtberg/Kuijper inversion on our own data: never accept a
   calibration because its reprojection RMS is low.
4. **Task-space vs parameter-space matters.**  At 25 views the task planner is
   0.193 mm vs 0.222 mm for the parameter A-optimal planner; more importantly,
   the task planner is the only strategy that improves monotonically with
   budget and wins every trial against the vendor plan.  D-optimal on raw
   parameters (`kalibr_param`) is no better than A-optimal and its determinant
   mixes px, rad and mm.
5. **AprilCal-style ERE is strong at small budgets but saturates.**  0.236 mm at
   8 views (best or near-best), 0.247 mm at 25 (no better than task-space).
   Its target-point reprojection error is a 2-D proxy for the 3-D task; once
   the volume is well covered the two diverge.
6. **Random and uniform are poor.**  0.54 mm and 0.46 mm at 8 views, 0.28 mm and
   (12-pose only) at 25: uniform coverage of the *pose* space is not uniform
   coverage of the *information* space.
7. **The effort penalty costs accuracy.**  `task_effort` is 0.222 mm vs 0.193 mm
   for `task_volume` at 25 views (and 0.277 vs 0.230 at 16).  The difference is
   inside the per-trial scatter but consistent in direction.  Production
   guidance therefore uses a **lexicographic** policy: keep candidates within
   10 % of the best predicted gain and choose the easiest of those, so effort
   breaks near-ties instead of trading away information.
8. **The coverage-first hybrid is competitive but has heavier tails.**  0.199 mm
   median at 25 (best median) but wins only 7/10 and has a wide CI; at 16 it is
   0.240 vs 0.230 for pure task selection.  Coverage-first is kept as the
   bootstrap (it guarantees the weak directions are excited); the steady-state
   selection is the task objective.

## 4. Guided session: progressive modes and stopping

The session (`calib/session.py`) was run closed-loop with the synthetic
operator: it chooses the next pose, the operator moves with 4 mm / 0.6°
positioning noise, the session applies the dwell/quality gates and decides when
to stop.  Two trials per mode, cap 26 accepted views
(`session_modes_small.json`):

| mode | accepted (median, range) | stop reached | 3-D recon median (range) mm | held-out 3-D (mm) | baseline rel err | rot err (°) | solve time (s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| quick | 18 (16–20) | 2/2 | 0.246 (0.090–0.401) | 0.361 | −0.012 % | 0.034 | 14.7 |
| standard | 24.5 (23–26) | 1/2 | 0.299 (0.205–0.392) | 0.413 | +0.012 % | 0.084 | 57.9 |
| high | 22 (20–24) | 0/2 | 0.297 (0.167–0.427) | 0.398 | +0.005 % | 0.073 | 51.4 |

**Honest reading.**  With two trials per mode the modes are not statistically
distinguishable: the per-scene variance (0.09–0.43 mm) dominates the
between-mode difference.  This is a *negative* result for the naive expectation
that more views always improve the estimate, and it is consistent with §3.2:
the extra views must be informative and the distortion model must stay
identifiable.  The reliable, measured behaviour is that the session
( a) rejects moving/blurred/redundant frames with an explanation,
(b) stops in `quick` and usually in `standard`, and
(c) never accepts a frame that fails the quality gates.
The mode thresholds in `session.MODES` are therefore offered as *operator
profiles*, not as an accuracy guarantee; the 10-trial offline comparison in §2–3
is the stronger evidence for the selection objective.  A production rollout
should re-measure the mode curve on hardware with a calibrated artefact.

## 5. Real-corpus consistency (not accuracy)

The 75-image vendor corpus was re-solved with the LM backend and the `auto`
localiser (`--solver lm --localize auto`):

* 22 views kept (3 rejected by the per-view consistency gate), 2626 markers;
* 0.329 px training RMS, 0.392 px held-out RMS, epipolar median 0.14 px;
* baseline 160.09 mm at 28.0 mm pitch (158.4 mm at 27.72 mm), i.e. the same
  1 % vendor-vs-nominal difference;
* split-half max |z| = 5.6 and one-view-drop stability max z = 12 for the
  right/tex intrinsics: the covariance does **not** explain the between-subset
  variation.  This is a model/data inconsistency (bias), not noise, and it is
  why the corpus calibration is reported as *unverified for physical accuracy*
  and cannot be accepted as a high-accuracy profile.

On synthetic data with the same solver and 25 views the stability gate passes
(max z = 0.44) and the stopping report passes, so the gate itself is calibrated;
the vendor corpus is the outlier.

## 6. Limitations

* Synthetic truth is *the same model family* as the estimator: it can falsify
  bugs and rank strategies, but it cannot prove physical accuracy.
* The 3-D recon metric evaluates a hypothetical point measurement with the
  estimated rig; it does not include plate manufacturing error, through-hole
  aperture bias or lens-model error beyond the simulated distortion.
* The vendor corpus cannot rank localisers below ~0.05 px (CCF-limited).
* No strategy here can detect a wrong plate pitch (§2 of the design doc).
* Operator effort is a model (150 mm, 25° = 1 unit), not measured human data.
* The 10 trials give medians with wide CIs; differences below ~0.05 mm should
  not be over-interpreted.
